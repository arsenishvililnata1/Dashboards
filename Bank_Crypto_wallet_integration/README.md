## 🎯 What Is This Dashboard?

What if Bank could turn **existing customer behavior into a new crypto revenue stream?**

The **Bank Crypto Integration Dashboard** explores exactly that.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Customer profiles
- Transaction behavior
- Existing crypto activity
- Investment behavior
- Mobile-banking engagement
- Potential crypto adoption
- Revenue scenarios
- Vendor economics
- Integration costs
- Profitability and ROI

Instead of looking at isolated metrics, the dashboard brings everything together into **one decision-making tool.**

### Key Business Questions

- 👥 How large is the potential customer base?
- ₿ How many customers already interact with crypto exchanges?
- 💰 How much crypto-related transaction volume exists today?
- 🎯 Who are the best potential customers for a crypto product?
- 📈 What could Bank's annual revenue look like under different adoption scenarios?
- 🧮 Would the integration actually be profitable?
- 🏦 Which vendor economics provide the best ROI?
- 🔮 How does the opportunity change under different transaction-frequency assumptions?

> **The goal is simple: turn raw banking data into a clear business case for crypto integration.**

## 💡 The Big Opportunity

The analysis starts with a simple observation:

> **Bank already has customers demonstrating crypto-related behavior.**

From a population of approximately **40,000 customers**:

- **7,654 customers** already have approved transactions with major crypto exchanges.
- This represents approximately **19.1%** of the customer base.
- Existing crypto-related transaction volume is approximately **$11.2M per month**.
- That translates to roughly **$135M in annual transaction volume**.

But the opportunity isn't limited to existing crypto users.

### 🎯 23,262 Potential Customers

The analysis identifies **23,262 potential customers** who are:

1. Not currently identified as active crypto users, **and**
2. Meet at least one strong engagement criterion:
   - Use Bank's Investment Module, **or**
   - Log into Mobile Banking at least **10 times per month**

This creates a potential conversion pool of approximately:

> ## **58.2% of the analyzed customer base**

This conversion pool represents the core growth opportunity explored throughout the dashboard.

#### 💰 Revenue Scenarios

The dashboard models four customer-adoption scenarios:

| Scenario | Adoption | Potential Customers | Approx. Annual Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 🟢 Low | **30%** | 6,979 | **~$1.46M** |
| 🔵 Conservative | **50%** | 11,631 | **~$2.43M** |

**🚀 Up to ~$4.86M Annual Revenue**

Under the aggressive scenario, assuming:

- **23,262 potential customers**
- **$876 average transaction**
- **1.67 transactions per month**
- **12 months**
- **2% revenue share**

the modeled annual revenue reaches approximately:

> ## **$4.86M per year**

⚠️ **Important:** This is **not a guaranteed forecast**. It is a scenario-based estimate designed to demonstrate the potential scale of the opportunity under different adoption assumptions.

#### 🔄 Transaction-Frequency Scenarios

Customer adoption isn't the only variable. The dashboard also models three transaction-frequency scenarios:

| Scenario | Transactions / Month | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative TX | **1.0** | Lower customer activity |
| Base TX | **1.67** | Based on observed December 2024 activity |
| Optimistic TX | **2.5** | Higher customer engagement |

This allows stakeholders to understand how sensitive the business case is to customer activity.

> **More customers + more transactions = significantly higher revenue potential.**

#### 🏦 Vendor Economics

Revenue alone doesn't make a business case. The dashboard therefore models multiple vendor cost structures. For each vendor, the analysis considers:

- Cost per transaction
- Annual fixed cost
- One-time setup cost
- Year 1 total cost, profit, and ROI
- Breakeven period
- Year 2+ total cost, profit, and ROI

This allows the business to compare not only *"Which vendor is cheapest?"* but rather:

> **"Which vendor produces the strongest economic outcome?"**

#### 📈 ROI Calculation

**Year 1** — includes the one-time setup cost in addition to recurring costs.

```text
Total Cost
Year 1 Total Cost =
    Annual Transactions × Cost per Transaction
    + Annual Fixed Cost
    + Setup Cost

Revenue
Annual Revenue =
    Potential Customers
    × Transactions per Month
    × 12
    × Average Transaction Value
    × Revenue Share

Profit
Year 1 Profit =
    Annual Revenue
    - Year 1 Total Cost

ROI
Year 1 ROI =
    Year 1 Profit
    / Year 1 Total Cost
    × 100
```

**Year 2+** — from Year 2 onward, the one-time setup cost is excluded.

```text
Total Cost
Year 2+ Total Cost =
    Annual Transactions × Cost per Transaction
    + Annual Fixed Cost

Profit
Year 2+ Profit =
    Annual Revenue
    - Year 2+ Total Cost

ROI
Year 2+ ROI =
    Year 2+ Profit
    / Year 2+ Total Cost
    × 100
```

This distinction is important because a vendor may appear less attractive in Year 1 due to setup costs while becoming significantly more profitable in subsequent years.

#### ⏱️ Breakeven Analysis

The dashboard also evaluates how quickly the integration can recover its initial investment.

A simplified breakeven calculation is:

```text
Breakeven Period =
    Initial Investment
    / Monthly Profit
```

The breakeven analysis helps answer:

> **How long would it take for the crypto integration to pay for itself?**

This provides an additional perspective beyond annual revenue and ROI.

#### 🎯 Potential Customer Logic

The potential-customer segment is based on the following logic:

```text
Potential Customer =
    Not an Active Crypto Customer
    AND
    (
        Investment Module User
        OR
        Mobile Banking Logins >= 10 / Month
    )
```

This approach intentionally focuses on behavioral signals rather than simply targeting the entire customer base.

## ⚠️ Assumptions & Limitations

The financial results presented in this dashboard are **scenario-based estimates, not guaranteed forecasts.**

**Key assumptions include:**

- Approximately 40,000 customers in the analyzed population
- 7,654 active crypto customers identified through exchange-related transaction behavior
- Approximately $11.2M monthly crypto-related transaction volume
- 23,262 potential customers identified through behavioral criteria
- Average transaction value of approximately $876
- Base transaction frequency of 1.67 transactions per month
- Revenue share assumption of 2%
- Adoption scenarios of 30%, 50%, 70%, and 100%
- Transaction-frequency scenarios of 1.0, 1.67, and 2.5 transactions per month

**Actual commercial results would depend on factors such as:**

- Product adoption
- Customer retention
- Pricing
- Regulatory requirements
- Compliance costs
- Vendor contracts
- Transaction mix
- Market conditions
- Operational costs
- Customer behavior after launch

Therefore, the model should be interpreted as a **business-case exploration and scenario analysis**, rather than a financial guarantee.
