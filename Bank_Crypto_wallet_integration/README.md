## 🎯 What Is This Dashboard?

What if BOG could turn **existing customer behavior into a new crypto revenue stream?**

The **BOG Crypto Integration Dashboard** explores exactly that.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Customer profiles
- Transaction behavior
- Existing crypto activity
- Investment behavior
- Mobile-banking engagement
- Potential crypto adoption
- Revenue scenarios
- Vendor economics
- Integration costs
- Profitability and ROI

Instead of looking at isolated metrics, the dashboard brings everything together into one decision-making tool.

### Key Business Questions

- 👥 How large is the potential customer base?
- ₿ How many customers already interact with crypto exchanges?
- 💰 How much crypto-related transaction volume exists today?
- 🎯 Who are the best potential customers for a crypto product?
- 📈 What could BOG's annual revenue look like under different adoption scenarios?
- 🧮 Would the integration actually be profitable?
- 🏦 Which vendor economics provide the best ROI?
- 🔮 How does the opportunity change under different transaction-frequency assumptions?

> **The goal is simple: turn raw banking data into a clear business case for crypto integration.**

# 💡 The Big Opportunity

The analysis starts with a simple observation:

> **BOG already has customers demonstrating crypto-related behavior.**

From a population of approximately **40,000 customers**:

- **7,654 customers** already have approved transactions with major crypto exchanges.
- This represents approximately **19.1%** of the customer base.
- Existing crypto-related transaction volume is approximately **$11.2M per month**.
- That translates to roughly **$135M in annual transaction volume**.

But the opportunity isn't limited to existing crypto users.

## 🎯 23,262 Potential Customers

The analysis identifies **23,262 potential customers** who are:

1. Not currently identified as active crypto users, and
2. Meet at least one strong engagement criterion:
   - Use BOG's Investment Module, **or**
   - Log into Mobile Banking at least **10 times per month**

This creates a potential conversion pool of approximately:

> # **58.2% of the analyzed customer base**

This conversion pool represents the core growth opportunity explored throughout the dashboard.

## 💰 Revenue Scenarios

The dashboard models four customer-adoption scenarios:

| Scenario | Adoption | Potential Customers | Approx. Annual Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 🟢 Low | **30%** | 6,979 | **~$1.46M** |
| 🔵 Conservative | **50%** | 11,631 | **~$2.43M** |
| 🟣 Moderate | **70%** | 16,283 | **~$3.40M** |
| 🔥 Aggressive | **100%** | 23,262 | **~$4.86M** |

## 🚀 Up to ~$4.86M Annual Revenue

Under the aggressive scenario, assuming:

- **23,262 potential customers**
- **$876 average transaction**
- **1.67 transactions per month**
- **12 months**
- **2% revenue share**

the modeled annual revenue reaches approximately:

> # **$4.86M per year**

⚠️ **Important:** This is **not a guaranteed forecast**.

It is a scenario-based estimate designed to demonstrate the potential scale of the opportunity under different adoption assumptions.

## 🔄 Transaction-Frequency Scenarios

Customer adoption isn't the only variable.

The dashboard also models three transaction-frequency scenarios:

| Scenario | Transactions / Month | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative TX | **1.0** | Lower customer activity |
| Base TX | **1.67** | Based on observed December 2024 activity |
| Optimistic TX | **2.5** | Higher customer engagement |

This allows stakeholders to understand how sensitive the business case is to customer activity.

> **More customers + more transactions = significantly higher revenue potential.**

# 🏦 Vendor Economics

Revenue alone doesn't make a business case.

The dashboard therefore models multiple vendor cost structures.

For each vendor, the analysis considers:

- Cost per transaction
- Annual fixed cost
- One-time setup cost
- Year 1 total cost
- Year 1 profit
- Year 1 ROI
- Breakeven period
- Year 2+ total cost
- Year 2+ profit
- Year 2+ ROI

This allows the business to compare not only:

> **"Which vendor is cheapest?"**

but rather:

> **"Which vendor produces the strongest economic outcome?"**

---

# 📈 ROI Calculation

## Year 1

Year 1 includes the **one-time setup cost** in addition to recurring costs.

### Total Cost

```text
Year 1 Total Cost =
    Annual Transactions × Cost per Transaction
    + Annual Fixed Cost
    + Setup Cost
```
